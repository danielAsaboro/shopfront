3Your orders and invoices

Export open purchase orders or supplier invoices as a CSV from your POS, inventory system or accountant, or the order-history report from Amazon Business. Each becomes a delivery the door checks off, so a receipt or a dispute names the order it was for. Re-importing the same file updates it.

CSV format

day,supplier,window_start,window_end,order_ref,carrier,tracking,items,amount 2026-10-21,Bay Mills,09:00,11:00,PO-1042,UPS,1Z999AA10123456784,"Flour 25kg x4",$212.40

Only day and supplier are needed. Other names work too: Order ID, PO number, Vendor, Seller Name, Expected delivery, Tracking number, Carrier Name & Tracking Number.
